The Mocha Muse Story

Founded in 2026 in Bidholi, Dehradun, Mocha Muse brings together small-batch Indian specialty coffee, botanical teas, and warm morning bakes in a calm sage-green space.

We partner directly with Chikmagalur estates for medium-dark Arabica roasts and serve every order in 100% plastic-free, compostable packaging.
